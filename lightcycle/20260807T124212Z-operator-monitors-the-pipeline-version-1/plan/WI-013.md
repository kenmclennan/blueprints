# WI-013 - A stacked row renders as one full-width cell

## What

When a row stacks, its continuation line spans the row rather than wrapping inside the flexible column, and it is indented by the design's continuation indent rather than by wherever the flexible column happens to start.

## Why this is separate from WI-008

WI-008's scenarios were frozen from the row-grid rules as they stood, and those rules were then amended twice - once to say the continuation line spans the row, and once to define the continuation indent and correct the floor. The frozen scenarios therefore assert the superseded rule verbatim: three of the four say the continuation is "indented to where it starts in the unstacked grid", which is the exact sentence the amendment removed, and the hierarchy's says "the row's own depth plus 2 characters", which the amendment replaced.

A build cannot satisfy both the amended design and its own frozen contract, and the contract is not the builder's to change. So this is not a defect in WI-008's delivery - it is a different mechanism, needing its own scenarios cut from the current design.

## Defined by

- [Design System Tokens](../design-system/design-system-tokens.md) - "Row grid conventions": the three column kinds, the paragraph beginning "A stacked row is not a grid row", the continuation-indent table, and "The floor".

## In scope

- The stacked-row rendering mechanism: one full-width cell holding two composed lines - the identifying fields padded to their atomic widths on the first, the prose on the second - rather than a grid row whose flexible column wraps.
- The continuation indent per the design's table: Priority List 6, Backlog 2, Hierarchy tab 6 plus the row's depth indent, Artifacts tab 2.
- The floor's second term, `continuation indent + flexible minimum`, which only becomes meaningful once the indent is defined.
- **Re-cutting the four stacked-row scenarios** in `priority-list-renders-current-work.feature`, `the-backlog-screen.feature`, `the-hierarchy-tab.feature` and `the-artifacts-tab.feature` from the amended design. Their current text asserts the superseded rule and cannot be satisfied alongside it.

## Out of scope

- Atomic column sizing, the breakpoint condition, and the floor's first term - WI-008 delivers these and they are correct.
- Any other column's contents, order or meaning.

## Done when

At every terminal width between the floor and the width at which rows stop stacking, no continuation line wraps mid-word, and each grid's continuation sits at the indent the design's table states. Verified on the actual composited frame at a width inside the band - 70 and 60 both produced one-fragment-per-line prose before this item.

## Notes from the codebase

`lightcycle/adapters/tui/row_grid.py` already computes `atomic_widths`, `stacked`, `floor` and a `flexible_width`; `compute_layout`'s stacked branch is where the corrected width belongs. `app.py`'s `_stacked_safe_width` exists specifically to discard that value and recompute the column-relative leftover - it is the thing this item removes, and it was written because the frozen scenarios require the atomic fields to remain real DataTable columns on the continuation line. A `DataTable` column's width applies to every line of a row, so the full-width mechanism cannot be reached by changing column widths alone.
