## WI-030: Hierarchy: root-down rendering + depth indentation

**Brief:** The Hierarchy tab's base rendering: resolve the node's actual root (its theme if one exists,
otherwise the top-level item itself) by walking the live `parent` chain, and render every descendant down
from there, indented by depth. **"Every descendant" includes both real, filed steps and, for whichever
item is currently in progress, its remaining planned-but-unfiled steps from WI-055** - the wireframe's own
`#hierarchy` example shows an item's full 8-step sequence even though only 4 steps had been filed at that
point (see WI-055's own Provenance/Source References); rendering only real rows would under-deliver
against every Hierarchy wireframe state, not just one.

### Acceptance Criteria

Copied verbatim from the source story:

- Given a node under a theme, when I open the hierarchy from it, then it shows that theme at the top with
  all its items and their steps below.
- Given a themeless item, when I open the hierarchy from it or one of its steps, then it shows that item
  as the root, with its steps below - no blank or missing theme row.
- Given the hierarchy is showing, when I look at any node, then its depth (theme/item/step) is visible by
  indentation.

### Source References

- **Section:** Context
- **Excerpt:** "Live-store check (2026-07-23): themes are optional in the model, and 165 of 250 items
  (66%) have no parent theme - most real work is themeless, root-level items. The tree can't assume a
  theme root. Every node already carries a `theme` field, but it isn't a shortcut for finding the root:
  for an item node the store derives it fresh from `parent` on every read, while for a step node it's a
  metadata column that ordinary step creation never populates... Root-detection has to walk the live
  `parent` chain (step to item, item to theme-or-none) rather than read `theme` off a step node."

### Dependencies

- WI-028, WI-055 (planned-step projection - real rows and projected rows both render in this tab).

### Constraints

- **Must walk the live `parent` chain, never read a step's own `theme` metadata field** - confirmed that
  field is only populated for item nodes (derived fresh from `parent` on read) and left unset by ordinary
  step creation for step nodes.
- Depth is bounded: theme > item > step is the entire chain (see WI-035's Constraints) - indentation
  logic does not need to handle arbitrary recursion.
- Real (filed) and planned (WI-055-projected) steps render in the same list, at the same indentation
  depth - a planned step is still conceptually a child of the same item, it simply has no real state/role/
  artifacts of its own yet (see WI-031's own Constraints for how the two are distinguished visually).

### Verification

- Pilot test: opening the hierarchy from a step under a themed item shows the theme at the top, the item
  indented once, its steps indented twice (or per whatever indentation scheme is chosen, consistently
  deeper than the item).
- Pilot test: opening the hierarchy from a themeless item shows that item as the top row with no blank
  theme placeholder.
- Pilot test: fixture matching the live-store finding (an item with no `parent` theme, whose `theme` field
  is nonetheless populated stale/incorrectly) still renders correctly rooted, proving the walk uses
  `parent`, not the `theme` field.

### Deliverable

The Hierarchy tab correctly resolves and renders the full root-down tree for both themed and themeless
nodes, with depth shown by indentation.
