# WI-003 - Footer status line and quitting

## What

The footer's status line carries every segment the design specifies - engine version, upgrade availability, and whether Claude is available to process work - and the dashboard can be quit.

## Defined by

- [wireframe-backlog.html](../wireframes/wireframe-backlog.md) - the same footer on the second screen, which is what makes it persistent rather than per-screen.
- [Design System Tokens](../design-system/design-system-tokens.md) - the footer's structure and which colours the alert styling draws from.
- [Keyboard Navigation Scheme](../design-system/keyboard-navigation-scheme.md) - the quit keys.

## Notes from the codebase

At `b2aa7b6` the footer is a one-line `StatusBar` in `lightcycle/adapters/tui/app.py` that already reports pool state and breaker state, polling on the same interval. Two facts about it matter here:

- **Both existing segments speak the engine's vocabulary, not the operator's, and neither carries its design treatment.** The breaker renders as open or closed where the design states what the operator sees; the pool segment renders as plain text where all four wireframes pair it with a glyph and a colour. Both are this item's work, and both stories' criteria are unmet until they land. The design wins.
- The reads themselves are done and locked by feature tests: `BreakerStatusUseCase` returns the breaker's state at rest, and `PoolRunningUseCase` reads the run lock without acquiring it - which is what keeps the TUI's read-only guarantee intact. A shipped read is not a delivered story.

The upgrade path already exists: `cli.py` calls `upgrade(__version__, check_only=True)` (`lightcycle/application/setup/upgrade.py`), and `__version__` is exported from `lightcycle/__init__.py`.

Where the design and the codebase disagree, the design wins.

## Open questions

- `[builder]` Whether the upgrade check runs once at launch or on the poll interval. The design states only that it degrades silently on failure; a check on every tick would reach past the local store repeatedly, which the quality attributes treat as the exceptional case.
