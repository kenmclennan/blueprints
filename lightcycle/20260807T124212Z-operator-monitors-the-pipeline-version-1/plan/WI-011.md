## WI-011: Needs-attention items pinned to top

**Brief:** Group inbox and blocked steps (the engine's `inbox` ∪ `blocked` lanes, merged into this
design's own "needs-attention" concept - not an engine lane) at the top of the priority list, visually
distinct.

### Acceptance Criteria

Copied verbatim from the source story:

- Given an item enters the inbox or blocked bucket, when the list next renders, then it appears in the
  needs-attention group at the top, differentiated by its own label/icon/colour.
- Given multiple needs-attention items exist, when I view the list, then they're grouped together above
  active and queued items, not interleaved.
- Given a row's title is longer than one line, when it doesn't fit, then it wraps to a second line rather
  than being truncated with an ellipsis.
- Given a needs-attention item, when it appears in the list, then its current step name is shown (e.g.
  'code-await-merge').
