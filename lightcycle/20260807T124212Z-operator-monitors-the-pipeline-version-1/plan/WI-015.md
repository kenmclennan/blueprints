## WI-015: Keep my selection on an item as it moves

**Brief:** The diff-based poll loop re-associates the selected item by identity to its new row position
on every poll, so a state change/re-sort never disturbs the selection. When the selected item leaves the
list entirely (e.g. becomes done), selection falls to the nearest remaining row rather than going empty.

### Acceptance Criteria

Copied verbatim from the source story:

- Given I have an item selected, when its state changes and it moves position in the sorted list, then my
  selection follows it to its new position.
- Given my selected item leaves the list entirely (e.g. becomes done), when that happens, then selection
  falls to the nearest remaining row rather than an empty selection.

### Dependencies

- WI-014.

### Constraints

- AC-1 (re-sort follows) is a natural consequence of WI-010's poll-diff mechanism, already
  identity-keyed - this item's real scope is AC-2, which needs the item's last-known row position
  retained as additional state, not just its id.

### Verification

- Pilot test: selecting an item, then simulating a state change that moves it in sort order, confirms
  selection follows to the new position.
- Pilot test: selecting an item, then simulating it leaving the list (e.g. transitioning to done),
  confirms selection lands on the nearest remaining row, not an empty selection.

### Deliverable

Selection reliably follows the tracked item across re-sorts and falls back sensibly when the item leaves
