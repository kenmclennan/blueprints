## Scroll the hierarchy with the keyboard

**Who:** Operator

**What:** Arrow keys scroll up and down through the fully-expanded tree, moving the selection between nodes.

**Why:** Consistent keyboard-only navigation - nothing needs expanding, since everything is already shown.

**Activity:** Inspect one item's journey - Understand a specific item end-to-end - its artifacts, child steps, and logs - to see what happened or why it's stuck.

**Step:** Explore the hierarchy

**Status:** ready

### Acceptance Criteria

- Given the hierarchy has more rows than fit on screen, when I press up/down, then the selection moves to the previous/next node, scrolling as needed.
- Given the selection is at the first or last node, when I press past the end, then it stays put rather than wrapping.
- Given the hierarchy is longer than one screen, when I press Ctrl+U or Ctrl+D, then the view jumps by roughly a full screen at a time.

- [wireframe-hub.html#hierarchy](../wireframes/wireframe-hub.md)

### Context

Scrolling isn't a plain clamp-and-shift of the row list: once the current node's ancestor scrolls out of its normal position, the wireframe pins that ancestor row to the top of the hierarchy body (its own row style, sitting above the scrolled rows) rather than letting it disappear - so the operator always sees which theme/item the current node belongs to no matter how deep the selection has scrolled. That pinned row is an extra element layered above the scrolling row set, not part of it, and needs to update independently as the selection crosses ancestor boundaries while scrolling.
