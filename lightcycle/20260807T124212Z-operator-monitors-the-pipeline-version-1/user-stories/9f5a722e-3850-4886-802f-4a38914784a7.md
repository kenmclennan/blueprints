## Return to the artifact list without losing position

**Who:** Operator

**What:** Closing an open artifact (text, URL, file, or list) returns to the artifact list at the same scroll/selection position.

**Why:** One level of back-navigation at a time, same contract as everywhere else.

**Activity:** Inspect one item's journey - Understand a specific item end-to-end - its artifacts, child steps, and logs - to see what happened or why it's stuck.

**Step:** View an artifact

**Status:** ready

### Acceptance Criteria

- Given I opened an artifact from the list, when I close it with Esc or ←, then the artifact list reappears with that artifact still selected.
