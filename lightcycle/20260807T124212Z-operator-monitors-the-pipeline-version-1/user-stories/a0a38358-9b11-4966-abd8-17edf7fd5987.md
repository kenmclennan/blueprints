## See when the step finishes while I'm watching

**Who:** Operator

**What:** If the worker completes while the log is open, the view shows that clearly rather than silently going stale.

**Why:** So I know whether I'm looking at a live process or a finished one.

**Activity:** Inspect one item's journey - Understand a specific item end-to-end - its artifacts, child steps, and logs - to see what happened or why it's stuck.

**Step:** Stream the current step's live log

**Status:** ready

### Acceptance Criteria

- Given the live log is open and the worker completes, when that happens, then the view clearly indicates the step has finished, not just going silent.
- Given the step has finished, when I look at the log, then it still shows the accumulated output up to completion, not cleared.

### References

- [wireframe-hub.html#log-finished - the finished indicator alongside the retained log](../wireframes/wireframe-hub.md)

### Context

Nothing in the store marks a step "finished" for this view to read directly - completion has to be inferred the same way the pool's own sweep infers it: by checking whether the worker's pid is still alive and its recorded start time still matches the live process (guarding against pid reuse), not from a stored status flag. A dead worker's entry in the workers registry does not disappear the instant it dies - it lingers until the pool's sweep cycle later prunes it - so "worker still listed but dead" and "worker gone from the registry" are two distinct, separately observable states, which is part of why AC-1 calls out "not just going silent" as insufficient.
