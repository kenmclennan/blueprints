## See needs-attention items pinned to the top

**Who:** Operator

**What:** Inbox and blocked items surface at the top of the priority list.

**Why:** So nothing needing me gets buried under routine activity.

**Activity:** Get oriented on the pipeline - See the whole pipeline at a glance - what's active, blocked, waiting on me, and queued - the moment it changes, as long as the dashboard is running.

**Step:** Review current work status

**Status:** ready

### Acceptance Criteria

- Given an item enters the inbox or blocked bucket, when the list next renders, then it appears in the needs-attention group at the top, differentiated by its own label/icon/colour.
- Given multiple needs-attention items exist, when I view the list, then they're grouped together above active and queued items, not interleaved.
- Given a row's title is longer than one line, when it doesn't fit, then it wraps to a second line rather than being truncated with an ellipsis.
- Given a needs-attention item, when it appears in the list, then its current step name is shown (e.g. 'code-await-merge').
