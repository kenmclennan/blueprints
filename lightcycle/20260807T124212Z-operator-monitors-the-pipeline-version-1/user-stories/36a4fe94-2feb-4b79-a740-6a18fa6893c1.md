## Scroll back through the live buffer

**Who:** Operator

**What:** Scroll up to see recent lines before the current tail, without stopping the live stream.

**Why:** To catch context I might have missed, without losing the live feed.

**Activity:** Inspect one item's journey - Understand a specific item end-to-end - its artifacts, child steps, and logs - to see what happened or why it's stuck.

**Step:** Stream the current step's live log

**Status:** ready

### Acceptance Criteria

- Given the live log is open, when I scroll up, then I see earlier lines from the current buffer without stopping the live tail.
- Given I've scrolled up, when a new line arrives, then it's added to the buffer but doesn't yank my scroll position back to the bottom.
- Given the buffer is longer than one screen, when I press Ctrl+U or Ctrl+D, then I move through it a full screen at a time instead of one line at a time.

- [wireframe-hub.html#active-log](../wireframes/wireframe-hub.md)

### Context

AC-3's Ctrl+U/Ctrl+D binding isn't a local choice - it's the map's global fast-scroll convention, defined in the Keyboard Navigation Scheme, and picked over PageUp/PageDown specifically because most Mac laptop keyboards have no dedicated Page keys, while Ctrl+U/Ctrl+D is available everywhere. The story's own cited wireframe state doesn't show this shortcut in its footer key hints at all, so that rationale isn't otherwise visible from what the story currently points to.
